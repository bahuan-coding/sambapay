# The cadence — how ownership runs

Owner: Hansraj Ojha (Cadence Owner). Approver: André Silva (Bahuan).
Companions: [`ownership-framework.plan.md`](ownership-framework.plan.md) · [`words.md`](words.md) · [`surface-map.md`](surface-map.md) · [`register-updates.md`](register-updates.md)

## The contract of every row

**Owner (1) · Backup (1) · Mandate (1 line) · Cadence · Evidence** — one owner per process and per
live front; unowned items are blockers in the pulse until they have a name.

## Daily — the chase (Hansraj Ojha, ten minutes, screen-driven)

- Open the Ownership tab and the Open work tab; chase only what the screen flags — unowned items,
  proposals awaiting assignment, items without movement.
- One message per owner per item. The screen is the source of truth; never memory.
- A new ask enters the registry the day it starts (owner + backup), before anything else is written
  about it.

## Weekly — the Ownership Review (30 minutes)

Agenda, in order:

1. **Unowned queue** — every item gets a name. Nothing is assigned silently.
2. **Stale items** — no movement for more than 7 days: the owner declares the blocker, or the stage
   moves.
3. **Escalations** — Owner → Hansraj Ojha (cadence) → André Silva (Bahuan) (Approver).
4. **Closures** — finished initiatives retire; the learning stays in the register.

## The standard (mature, no ceremony)

- No update in two consecutive reviews = escalation, with consequences. Short and factual; the
  screen carries the evidence.
- The process owner owns the **HOW**; the DRI owns the **OUTCOME** until it closes.
- Nothing is decided in private: the register is the record.

## The first Monday — 12 Oct 2026

Hansraj Ojha assigns the Open Queue (the committee's recommendations sit on the Ownership panel);
the register follows the assignment the same day.
