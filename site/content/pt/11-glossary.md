<section class="beat beat-refuse">
<h2>O que recusamos</h2>
<p>Não pegamos os merchants dos nossos clientes. Dois nichos, sem conflito de interesse.</p>
</section>

<section class="beat beat-craft">
<dl class="ledger">
<dt>Adquirente</dt>
<dd>The licensed company that accepts card payments on behalf of merchants and settles the money to them.</dd>
<dt>Barrier to entry</dt>
<dd>The cost of holding compliance and the full structure of a regulated institution in these markets; we do that work so the client gets a local commercial policy.</dd>
<dt>BIN</dt>
<dd>Bank Identification Number: the first digits of a card number, identifying the issuing bank and the country of issuance. International BIN acceptance means the rail authorises cards whose BINs were issued outside the acquiring country.</dd>
<dt>Volume agregado</dt>
<dd>The book of many merchants together; that book is why a local merchant gets a commercial policy it could not get alone.</dd>
<dt>Portador</dt>
<dd>The person an issuer has issued a card to. Our priority volume is European and American cardholders, identified by those issuing BINs.</dd>
<dt>Banco Central do Brasil</dt>
<dd>The regulator of payments in Brazil; grants payment-institution and cross-border authorisations.</dd>
<dt>Conflito de interesse</dt>
<dd>Taking a client's merchants or disputing a client's accounts. We do not. Local merchants on the Website Factory are another niche.</dd>
<dt>Cross-border</dt>
<dd>Selling in a country other than the merchant's own.</dd>
<dt>Digitalização</dt>
<dd>Standing a local merchant, or a client's Brazil store, up as a digital business, case by case, through the Website Factory: from the CNPJ through operational management with the local entities, until payout.</dd>
<dt>Conexão direta</dt>
<dd>A live rail to a local acquirer in the country, through our own entity or a partner: the presence we offer so a business that does not have access to a cheap market can use the local commercial policy.</dd>
<dt>DPO</dt>
<dd>Data protection officer, the LGPD contact; dpo@sambapay.tech.</dd>
<dt>Escala horizontal</dt>
<dd>Growing volume by integrating several acquirers side by side in a market, so no single rail limits us or concentrates the risk.</dd>
<dt>Infrastructure</dt>
<dd>Our business model: we provide the always-on payment system, the local rails, and the Website Factory.</dd>
<dt>KYB</dt>
<dd>Know your business: verifying a merchant company, its owners and its risk before onboarding.</dd>
<dt>LGPD</dt>
<dd>Brazil's personal-data protection law.</dd>
<dt>Política comercial local</dt>
<dd>Local prices, local approval and local rules, as a local merchant would get them.</dd>
<dt>Local entity</dt>
<dd>An acquirer, bank, PIX rail or FX partner in a given country, or our own regulated company there.</dd>
<dt>Merchant local</dt>
<dd>A business in a geography where we have structure, digitised through the Website Factory. Entrepreneurs and service providers in the digital economy who want online payments. They use our capture channels as a local player. Not a client's merchant.</dd>
<dt>Merchant</dt>
<dd>A business that sells and gets paid on our rails: a local merchant on the Website Factory, or a merchant who arrives through a client. We do not take a client's merchants.</dd>
<dt>Merchant of Record</dt>
<dd>The company that legally sells to the shopper and carries the merchant risk and the reserve; today an operating partner.</dd>
<dt>Facilitador de pagamento</dt>
<dd>Visa's name for a company that onboards merchants under an acquirer agreement. What we operate as today, on the partner Merchant of Record. Distinct from sub-acquirer.</dd>
<dt>Instituição de pagamento</dt>
<dd>A company authorised by the Central Bank of Brazil to provide payment services. What we take after sub-acquiring is live. Grant date is the Central Bank's.</dd>
<dt>Rail</dt>
<dd>Any connection through which a payment or a settlement moves: an acquirer, PIX, a bank, a remittance path.</dd>
<dt>Conciliação</dt>
<dd>Tying the operator's file to our ledger, line by line, so the day's figure is right.</dd>
<dt>Scheme</dt>
<dd>A card network: Visa, Mastercard.</dd>
<dt>Settlement</dt>
<dd>The acquirer paying out the money from authorised transactions, on its window.</dd>
</dl>
</section>
