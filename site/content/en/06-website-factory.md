<section class="beat beat-affirm">
<p class="lead">The Website Factory is a capture rule, not an offer rule.</p>
</section>

<!-- src: company-os/governance/decision-log.md § paid-traffic performance channel -->

<section class="beat beat-fact">
<p>Local merchants come from a paid-traffic performance channel, a channel already used in the regions where we already have local structure. Never by taking a client's merchant, never disputing with our client's sales.</p>

<!-- src: welcome-kit/01-why-we-exist.md § Two niches, no conflict of interest -->

<p>Local merchants are a second public. They use our capture channels as a local player, on that geography's cheap commercial policy, because of aggregated volume. They do not access the same markets as the large foreigners our clients bring.</p>

<!-- src: welcome-kit/02-what-we-do.md § One service, three parts -->

<p>In each geography where we have structure, the factory runs from the CNPJ through operational management with the local entities, until payout. A client's Brazil store uses the same factory.</p>
</section>

<!-- src: .cursor/skills/sambapay-ceo/references/entrepreneurship-committee.md § We only exist because we never compete with our clients -->

<section class="beat beat-refuse">
<h2>What we refuse</h2>
<p>We do not take our clients' merchants. We do not dispute their accounts. Two niches, no conflict of interest.</p>
<p>We only exist because we never compete with our clients.</p>
</section>

<!-- src: company-os/governance/decision-log.md § We enable rails -->

<section class="beat beat-craft">
<p>We enable rails. The factory digitises local merchants on our own capture channels, as a local player, on the local commercial policy.</p>
</section>
