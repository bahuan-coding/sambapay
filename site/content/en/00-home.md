# SambaPay

<span class="status-pill">Institutional site · building</span>

<p class="lead">We open local rails in Latin America so money that already circulates in this economy can be used at local prices.</p>

Built in Brazil, by Brazilians, for markets that are hard to enter. The bureaucracy of these markets is the difficulty we took on. It is our expertise.

**The system is new. What it knows is not.** More than R$ 3 billion was processed before the system we run today existed. The volume is not the argument. What it taught is.

We do not take our clients' merchants. We do not dispute their accounts. Two niches. No conflict of interest.

This site is the public face of the same story as our Welcome Kit. Pages will appear here as they are settled. Nothing on this site names a counterparty, a target, or a strategic discussion.
