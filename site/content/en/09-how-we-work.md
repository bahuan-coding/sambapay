<section class="beat beat-affirm">
<p class="lead">Availability is the product. Speed of a ticket is not the product.</p>
</section>

<section class="beat beat-fact">
<ol>
<li>Maximum transparency. The first job is to organise what we have already discovered, as fast as possible.</li>
<li>Clear roles. Every one of us has a title and owns named processes, so an acquirer or a client knows exactly who runs what.</li>
<li>Always on. The engine is not allowed to go down. Traffic runs twenty-four hours a day, every day, from everywhere. An incident is reported the hour it appears.</li>
<li>Availability is the product. The client counts on the quality of this system. That is why a client lasts. Speed of a ticket is not the product.</li>
<li>Reconciliation first. The day starts with the right number. Nothing else is trusted until the book ties.</li>
<li>Open is honest. We do not hide gaps; we close them.</li>
<li>We do not compete with our clients. We do not take their merchants and we do not dispute their accounts. Local merchants on the Website Factory are another niche. If a task is not the rails, the Website Factory, or settlement, we question it. Two niches, no conflict of interest.</li>
<li>Diligent from day zero. Every merchant is screened before any acquirer sees it. Every acquirer's checklist is complete before go-live. Speed never buys an exception.</li>
<li>Acquirers first. We have a strong relationship with our partners, and with our acquirers above all. That relationship is our greatest focus, and our obsession.</li>
</ol>
</section>
