<p class="lead">One service, three parts.</p>
</section>

<section class="beat beat-fact">
<p>Rails connect a client's volume to local acquiring in Latin America, on a local commercial policy. The Website Factory digitises local merchants in the geographies where we have structure. Settlement takes the money collected locally out to the client.</p>
</section>

<!-- src: welcome-kit/02-what-we-do.md § What we do not do -->

<section class="beat beat-refuse">
<h2>What we refuse</h2>
<p>We do not take our clients' merchants. We do not dispute their accounts. We do not do fulfilment or shipping. Only the rails, the Website Factory, and the settlement that takes money out. Two niches, no conflict of interest.</p>
</section>
