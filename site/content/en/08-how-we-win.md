<section class="beat beat-fact">
<h2>Five reasons a client chooses us</h2>
<ol>
<li>Local commercial policy, on thin margins. Once we have local infrastructure, the commercial policy applied to a thin-margin client is what makes the difference.</li>
<li>Latin America, on purpose. We put the company here because these geographies are very cost-efficient. Negotiation points in a cheap market are the strategy.</li>
<li>European and American cardholders, by issuing BIN. Our priority is always to give those cardholders access to local commercial policy. Europe and the United States hold the largest cardholder bases in the world, identified by those issuing BINs. Local cost in Latin America; that volume from those BINs.</li>
<li>The barrier. Compliance is hard to hold. So is the full structure a regulated institution needs. We do that work. We give our clients access to a local commercial policy. That is a severe barrier to entry. The bureaucracy of these markets is the difficulty we took on. It is our expertise.</li>
<li>Intelligence on strategic pricing. We are focused on this. Our expertise is to develop intelligence on the processes of the players who hold the most strategic pricings in the world.</li>
</ol>
</section>
