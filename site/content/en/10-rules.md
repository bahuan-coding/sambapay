<!-- src: welcome-kit/08-rules-we-live-by.md § 1. We are regulated -->

<section class="beat beat-affirm">
<p class="lead">Today a partner is the Merchant of Record and carries the risk. We operate as the payment facilitator.</p>
</section>

<!-- src: welcome-kit/08-rules-we-live-by.md § 2. The engine stays up -->

<section class="beat beat-fact">
<p>The engine is not allowed to go down. Traffic runs twenty-four hours a day, every day, from everywhere. An incident is reported the hour it appears.</p>

<!-- src: welcome-kit/08-rules-we-live-by.md § 3. Card data is radioactive (PCI DSS) -->

<p>Card numbers exist only tokenised. Never in email, chat, a spreadsheet or a screenshot. If you see a card number where it should not be, stop.</p>

<!-- src: welcome-kit/08-rules-we-live-by.md § 4. Know the merchant before anyone else does (KYB) -->

<p>No merchant reaches an acquirer before we have checked who it is, who owns it, and whether any person is politically exposed or sanctioned. The checklist is complete before go-live. Speed never buys an exception.</p>

<!-- src: welcome-kit/08-rules-we-live-by.md § 5. Anti-money-laundering -->

<p>We never onboard what we cannot explain: the business, the owners, the source of volume. Anything suspicious is reported.</p>

<!-- src: welcome-kit/08-rules-we-live-by.md § 6. Personal data (LGPD) -->

<p>Shoppers' and merchants' personal data is used only for the payment and kept only as long as needed. Requests from data subjects go to dpo@sambapay.tech.</p>

<!-- src: welcome-kit/08-rules-we-live-by.md § 7. Card scheme rules -->

<p>Visa and Mastercard rules bind us through the acquirers. Merchant category codes are honest. A letter from an acquirer is the moment that tells whether this business works. A letter answered the hour it arrives. The facts arrive as soon as they exist.</p>

<!-- src: welcome-kit/08-rules-we-live-by.md § 8. Money moves only when the book ties -->

<p>Reconciliation first. No remittance, release or payout before the day's figure ties.</p>
</section>

<!-- src: welcome-kit/08-rules-we-live-by.md § 9. What each of us never does -->

<section class="beat beat-refuse">
<h2>What we refuse</h2>
<ul>
<li>Sends card data outside the place where it is tokenised.</li>
<li>Treats an engine drop as something to mention later.</li>
<li>Promises a merchant a go-live date before its checklist is done.</li>
<li>Moves money that has not been reconciled.</li>
<li>Takes a client's merchants or disputes a client's accounts. Two niches, no conflict of interest.</li>
<li>Hides a gap.</li>
</ul>
</section>

<section class="beat beat-craft">
<p>We will be a sub-acquirer: own merchant IDs under the acquirers, not yet a payment institution. The grant date is the Central Bank's, not ours to promise. Everything we promise a merchant must fit what we are licensed to do today.</p>
</section>
