## One service, three parts

**Rails.** We provide infrastructure that connects a client's volume to local acquiring in Latin America with a local commercial policy: local prices, local payment methods (cards, instant payments such as PIX, local instruments) and the best approval possible. This is where the capacity of a client's checkout actually grows: a market it could not reach alone becomes a market it can price for. Clients can bring their own payment methods to connect locally. We integrate several acquirers per market, side by side, so volume scales horizontally and no single rail limits us. We put structure in the country, or we connect with partners, and we hold a direct connection to local acquirers. Local signup gathers the client's data, delivers it to the local partners, and helps with the licences, the regulations and the requirements of regulated institutions, so the approval has a better chance. On that direct connection the client does not meet each requirement alone: it uses the volume of our local commercial policy and enters the competitive market. That is why the structure exists: so the client has a presence in a cheap market and uses the money that circulates in that economy, at local prices. We take production API keys from those acquirers and deliver them to the core team. The integration is deployed on PaySecure's white label for the client. We configure and reconcile on that white label, everything the PaySecure system already does.

**Website Factory.** An orchestration swarm of AI and product managers specialised in each business. They produce specifically for each digitisation case. In each geography where we have structure, we serve local merchants who want to digitise in that economy: from the CNPJ through operational management with the local entities, until payout. They use our capture channels as a local player, on the cheap commercial policy of that geography. We are online enablers for that local merchant. The business brings its stock: a niche marketplace, or stock shared by physical shops. The digitised online store includes the card terminal for online sales. The local client in each market has this included. We do not charge a marketplace fee. A client's Brazil store uses the same factory. Open: the named product managers in the swarm; the commercial owner.

**Settlement.** Money collected locally reaches PaySecure with the fewest hops, by one of two paths. The partner path: local PIX, then the partner's tokenized-PIX rail, then the PaySecure balance. The on-ramp path we ran as A55 Payments: same-day PIX to an OTC desk, USDC into our own wallet, USDC on to PaySecure, at a cost of a few hundredths of a percent against PTAX. Sridhar helps André Silva obtain a Power of Attorney to re-establish the FX contact and contract so settlements can start. Open: the Power of Attorney in hand, the contract signed, and which path carries which volume.

## The PaySecure deal

SambaPay is the rebrand of A55 Payments and part of PaySecure (paysecure.net), a global payments platform. The agreement is simple: PaySecure brings the volume; SambaPay is its market enabler in Latin America and opens the local rails. We run on PaySecure's white label. We inherit its PCI structure and its commercial policies in other markets. A client whose business has more than one nationality gets one system that already has local entities and teams in those markets. That is common in this industry, and it is value. PaySecure has been our client for years. We have already been through several cycles of growth together. That happened because the system is stable. Market enablement is the service we owe them, and the three parts above are how we deliver it. Brazil first, Mexico and Colombia next. Pricing and features are a separate conversation from volume.

## What we do not do

We do not take our clients' merchants. We do not dispute their accounts. We do not do fulfilment or shipping. Only the rails, the Website Factory, and the settlement that takes money out.

## Why the client stays

Availability is the product. The client counts on the quality of this system. It stays up. That is why the relationship lasts. PaySecure is the proof: years as a client, several cycles of growth, because the system is stable.

We have a strong relationship with our partners, and with our acquirers above all. That relationship is our greatest focus, and our obsession.
