# 01 · Why We Exist

Status: Settled

## What we were hired to do

PaySecure brings the flow. We create the connectivity that flow needs in Latin America. That is the whole agreement: PaySecure is the demand side, SambaPay is the supply side, and what we supply is not a connection. It is a local commercial policy, delivered as infrastructure.

Market enablement is the service. It is how we serve PaySecure. It was the premise and the requirement of the system from the first day we served them.

## Two niches, no conflict of interest

We do not take our clients' merchants and we do not dispute their accounts.

**Clients** are companies with international traffic. PaySecure is the first. We do market enablement and orchestration for them. Clients can bring their own payment methods to connect locally in Latin America. They can have payment clients of their own. Those books grow only when operational stability is this high.

**Local merchants** sit in each geography where we have structure. The Website Factory digitises entrepreneurs and service providers who want online payments and a commercial policy that maximises sales margin. They use our capture channels as a local player, on that geography's cheap commercial policy, because of aggregated volume. They do not access the same markets as the large foreigners our clients bring.

## Why a local commercial policy is the product

In most countries in this region, more payment traffic runs on local methods than on the big global brands. A business in Europe or the United States that wants to sell here does not need another gateway. It needs local prices, a local signup and a direct connection. Local signup gathers the client's data, delivers it to the local partners, and helps with the licences, the regulations and the requirements of regulated institutions, so the approval has a better chance. On a direct connection the client does not meet each requirement alone: it uses the volume of our local commercial policy and enters the competitive market. That work is a severe barrier to entry.

We offer a local connection as a presence for businesses that do not have access to cheap markets. We create a local structure in the country, or we connect with partners, and we hold a direct connection to local acquirers. The structure exists so the client can use the money that circulates in that economy, at local prices.

## Always-on pay technology

We are a paytech: a fintech specialised in payments. A focused system is an efficient system. The business model is to provide infrastructure. The culture is obsessively online: the engine is not allowed to go down. It has years of history without unavailability, carrying traffic twenty-four hours a day, every day, from everywhere. The service is consumed as a URL: a data-centre-level delivery for the payments segment, on PaySecure's white label. The live book in September is zero. We go to production with DD1 Cielo for volumes. We open DD2 in parallel. 1 October is day one of October volume.

## The system is new. What it knows is not

More than R$ 3 billion was processed before the system we run today existed. The volume is not the credential. What it taught is. Reconciliation first, an engine that is not allowed to go down, several acquirers side by side, a letter answered the hour it arrives: none of those are values we chose in a room. They are what that volume cost us to learn.

The system we run today is new, and it is new because of that. The architecture concept persisted, and new technology was absorbed natively, because the people who designed it already knew which part breaks first. The continuity is not the code. It is the team, and the culture that came out of processing that volume.

## Why it matters

Margin mechanics in these markets have not changed in a long time and will not change soon. What can change is who gets access. Without a local commercial policy, cross-border businesses are never truly included in the markets they want to serve. That exclusion is the problem we exist to remove. For the client two numbers decide everything: approval and rate. Approval times margin is what we move.

## Our mission

Grow the capacity of our clients' checkouts into markets they cannot reach alone. Local rails, local prices, the best approval we can get, through local entities that are regulated and PCI compliant, with every interface to those entities handled by us, on an engine that stays up. Digitise local merchants in those same geographies through the Website Factory.

We spread the load on purpose: several payment methods, instant payments, cards and local instruments, several acquirers side by side. Risk is limited by design, not by luck.
